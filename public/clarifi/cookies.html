Cookie Policy

How this site uses cookies and similar technologies under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and the UK GDPR. Last reviewed: 2026.

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device. This page explains which cookies the Clarifi site uses, why, and how you stay in control. It sits alongside our Privacy Notice, which covers personal data more broadly.

1. Nothing non-essential loads before you consent

This site is deny-by-default. When you first arrive, no analytics cookies and no advertising or marketing cookies are set. We do not load any analytics script, tracking pixel or tag until you make an active choice on the cookie banner. If you reject, or simply never choose, nothing non-essential runs — and your choice is remembered on your device so we do not ask again on every visit.

2. The categories we use

CategoryWhat it does
Essential Strictly necessary for the site to work — for example, remembering your cookie choice itself so we honour it. These do not require consent and cannot be switched off.
Analytics & marketing Help us understand how the site performs so we can improve it, and — where enabled — measure marketing campaigns. These load only after you accept. Reject and they never run.

3. The tools behind these categories

When (and only when) you accept, the following may be loaded:

  • HubSpot analytics — helps us see how the site is used and connects an enquiry to the pages that led to it. HubSpot is our CRM and marketing platform and stores data with EU data residency.
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag and Meta Pixel — advertising and measurement pixels that would only ever load after you consent. These are not currently active on the site; they will only run once the relevant advertising accounts are provisioned, and even then only behind your consent.

4. How to change your choice

You can change your mind at any time:

  • Clear this site’s data in your browser settings, then reload the page — the cookie banner will reappear and you can choose again.
  • Use your browser’s own cookie and privacy controls to block or delete cookies for this site.
  • Email us at hello@haim8.com if you would like help.

Rejecting analytics and marketing cookies does not affect your ability to read the site, request a resource, or book a call.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the tools we use or the law change. The version on this page is always current, and the review date above tells you when it last changed.
