Partner questions

The things partners ask first.

Is it genuinely my brand the client sees?

Yes. The client portal is white-labeled — your logo and identity across the dashboards, summaries and PDF exports. To your clients, it's your reporting product.

Can different clients see each other's data?

No. The platform is multi-tenant by design: each client gets an isolated space and sees only their own numbers. That isolation is what makes a white-label resale safe.

How much development do we have to do?

None per client. The platform is multi-tenant and white-label out of the box, so you stand it up once and onboard clients into it — there's no per-client build on your side.

What does it cost?

Partner terms are tailored to the size of your client base and discussed on the call. Pricing on application — there's no commitment to find out whether the numbers stack up for you.

Is the data defensible enough to put my name on?

The reporting is built on timestamped, verifiable data and has already been independently verified at the sharp end of live operations — including an 89% recycling rate verified at a major UK festival. It also gives your clients audit-ready support for ISO 14001 reporting.
