Privacy Notice

How HAIM8 Ltd collects and uses your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. Last reviewed: 2026.

This notice explains what personal data we collect when you use this Clarifi site, why we collect it, the lawful bases we rely on, who processes it on our behalf, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. Clarifi is the operational and sustainability reporting platform provided by HAIM8 Ltd.

1. Who we are

HAIM8 Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. We are a company registered in England & Wales, company number 17177572, based in London, United Kingdom. You can reach us about anything in this notice at hello@haim8.com.

2. The data we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us through the enquiry and content-request forms on this site, together with a small amount of context that helps us understand where an enquiry came from. Specifically:

  • Your name — so we can address you properly.
  • Your work email address — so we can reply and, where you have asked, deliver the guide or resource you requested.
  • Your company or organisation.
  • Your job title.
  • Your sector (for example events and venues, or facilities and waste management).
  • Your organisation’s employee band (an approximate size range).
  • Your buying trigger — the reason prompting your enquiry, such as an upcoming tender or a compliance deadline.
  • Campaign source information (UTM parameters) — the referral, campaign and content tags attached to the link you arrived through, so we know which activity brought you to us.

We do not ask for special-category data, and we ask you not to include sensitive personal details in free-text fields.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful bases

Under the UK GDPR we must have a lawful basis for each use of your data. The table below sets out what we do and the basis we rely on.

PurposeLawful basis
Responding to your enquiry and following up about Clarifi in a business-to-business context Legitimate interests — it is in our and your interest for us to reply to a business enquiry you initiated. We balance this against your rights and you can object at any time.
Delivering the specific guide, playbook or resource you requested Performance of your request / legitimate interests — you asked us to send it.
Sending you marketing about Clarifi and related HAIM8 Ltd services Consent — we only add you to marketing where you have opted in, and you can withdraw at any time.

4. Who processes your data

We use HubSpot as our customer relationship management (CRM) and marketing platform. HubSpot acts as a data processor on our behalf and stores the data you submit on infrastructure with EU data residency. HubSpot processes your data only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and contact data for as long as we are in active contact with you or your organisation about Clarifi, and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can pick up the conversation again if you return. Where our relationship becomes inactive, we review and delete or anonymise data that we no longer need. If you ask us to erase your data, we will do so unless we are required to keep it for a legal or accounting reason.

6. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data where there is no overriding reason to keep it.
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including objecting to direct marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@haim8.com and we will respond within the statutory time limit. You can unsubscribe from our marketing at any time using the one-click unsubscribe link in every marketing email, or by emailing us. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the UK’s supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

7. Cookies and tracking

This site loads no analytics or advertising cookies until you accept them. How we use cookies, and how to change your choice, is set out in our Cookie Policy.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our practices or the law evolve. The version on this page is always the current one, and the review date above tells you when it last changed.
