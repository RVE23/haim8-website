HAIM8 LTD — Privacy Notice

Effective: 18 May 2026  ·  Version: 1.0  ·  Controller: HAIM8 LTD, company number 17177572 (England & Wales), registered office 24 Sherrick Green Road, London NW10 1LD  ·  Privacy contact: privacy@haim8.com

This notice explains how HAIM8 LTD (“HAIM8”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data as an independent data controller under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Scope. This notice covers personal data we handle for our own purposes — operating the business, dealing with customers and suppliers, providing this website, and meeting legal obligations. When we build and host software platforms for our customers, we act as a data processor for those customers’ data, not as a controller; that processing is governed by the customer’s own privacy policy (e.g. each customer’s in-app Privacy Policy) and by the Data Processing Agreement we sign with that customer. This notice does not change those processor obligations.

Who we are and how to contact us

HAIM8 LTD is a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 17177572) with registered office at 24 Sherrick Green Road, London NW10 1LD, United Kingdom. We design, build, and operate software products for small and mid-size organisations.

For any question about this notice, to exercise a data-protection right, or to raise a concern, please contact us at privacy@haim8.com.

What personal data we collect, and why

2.1 Business contacts (customers, prospects, suppliers, partners)

When you correspond with us about our services or work with us on an engagement, we collect: name, business email address, phone number, employer, role, and the content of our exchanges (emails, meeting notes, contract details). We use this to deliver our services, manage the commercial relationship, raise and collect invoices, respond to enquiries, and keep records we are legally required to keep.

2.2 Website visitors (haim8.com)

When you visit haim8.com we automatically receive standard request data — IP address, user agent, page requested, referring URL, and timestamp — logged by our hosting provider to operate the site, detect abuse, and produce aggregate, non-identifying usage information. haim8.com does not set non-essential cookies and does not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

2.3 Recipients of our emails, invoices, and contract documents

We retain copies of correspondence and documents we send and receive (proposals, contracts, invoices, change orders, support tickets), which include the personal data of the people named in those documents. We use this to perform our contracts, evidence what was agreed, and meet record-keeping obligations under company and tax law.

2.4 Job applicants and candidates

If you apply to work with us, we collect the personal data you provide in your application (CV, contact details, work history, references) plus any notes we make during interview. We use this to assess your suitability and to communicate with you about the role.

2.5 Operational security data

We log access to our internal systems and to the platforms we operate (sign-in events, IP addresses, security alerts). We use this to investigate incidents, prevent unauthorised access, and meet our obligations under contracts and Data Protection Laws.

Some of our recipients are based outside the United Kingdom. Transfer Impact Assessments are carried out where required.

How we keep personal data secure

  • Access to systems containing personal data is restricted to people who need it for their role and is protected by multi-factor authentication where supported.
  • Data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Data at rest is encrypted by our hosting and storage providers (Google Workspace, Vercel, and any customer platform’s underlying provider).
  • We carry professional indemnity and cyber-liability insurance with Hiscox under our active business insurance policy.
  • We respond to suspected personal-data breaches without undue delay and, where required, notify the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) within 72 hours of becoming aware.

Your rights under the UK GDPR

You have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you (Article 15).
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data (Article 16).
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data where one of the grounds in Article 17 applies.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data (Article 18).
  • Portability — ask us to provide your data in a structured, commonly-used, machine-readable format where Article 20 applies.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing (Article 21).
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal (Article 7(3)).
  • Automated decision-making — we do not currently make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@haim8.com. We will respond within one month of receiving a valid request, in line with Article 12 UK GDPR.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data or your request, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, by telephone on 0303 123 1113, or by post to the Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF.

Cookies and similar technologies

haim8.com uses only strictly-necessary technical storage (such as a preference for light or dark theme, where applicable). It does not set advertising, profiling, or third-party analytics cookies, and it does not require a cookie banner under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 as currently published.

Customer platforms we operate may set additional cookies as described in that platform’s own privacy policy — for example, an authentication cookie for signed-in users, or an opt-in analytics cookie where the platform’s customer has chosen to enable product analytics. Those cookies are not set on haim8.com.
